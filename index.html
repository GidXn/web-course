Ask Mintlify

What is mintlify?

Mintlify is a service that aids in creating elegant and engaging API documentation. It is designed specifically for developers, utilizing MDX to power its content, which resides beside the codebase. This ensures effortless maintenance and optimization for user engagement.

Mintlify comes with a collection of built-in components that make your documentation appealing without the need for additional formatting.
