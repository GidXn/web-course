About the author

Mykhailo — builds small, precise tools for Windows

Comfortable in Windows environments and scripting, with a habit of tinkering until a setup is exactly right rather than just good enough.

A little background

Most of the work here is hands-on and practical: scripting fixes for real Windows quirks, and customizing a desktop down to the smallest detail rather than leaving it at defaults.

That shows up in projects like reworking a Rainmeter skin's time display to run on a 24-hour clock with proper localized day and month names, or writing a self-elevating batch script that handles UAC prompts cleanly instead of asking twice.

The common thread is control — over how a clock reads, how a script asks for permissions, how a desktop actually looks day to day.
